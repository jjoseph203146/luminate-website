WHAT A CONTRACTOR SITE NEEDS

A trade website has one job: help someone who's already interested trust you enough to call or ask for a quote. Everything on it should serve that.

WHAT A CONTRACTOR SITE NEEDS

Your work is the proof. Your website should show it.

✦

A gallery of past jobs

Finished projects, organized by service, so homeowners can see the kind of work you do before they call. Photos from your phone work fine.

✦

One-tap calling on every page

Many people looking for a contractor are on their phone. Your number stays one tap away the whole time.

✦

A quote request that asks the right questions

Project type, location, and timeline up front, so you can tell which jobs are worth a site visit before you drive out.

✦

A clear page for each service

Separate pages for what you actually do — roofing, decks, irrigation, repairs — so customers find the right one fast.

✦

The areas you cover

Augusta, Evans, Martinez, Grovetown, North Augusta, or wherever you work — listed plainly, so nobody has to ask.

✦

Trust signals up front

Licensing, insurance, warranties, and reviews where customers see them, not buried on a back page.

FAQ

Common questions from contractors

Rather talk it through? Call (762) 227-9287 or email us.

How much does a contractor website cost?▾

Launch sites start at $750 and Growth sites start at $1,500. If you offer several services or want a project gallery, Growth is usually the better fit. You'll get a written quote before any work begins. Optional Website Care is $50/mo.

I don't have professional photos. Is that a problem?▾

No. Clear phone photos of finished jobs work well — real work is more convincing than stock photos. Send what you have and we'll select and organize the best ones.

Can customers request a quote or book online?▾

Yes. Every site includes a contact form and click-to-call. Growth sites add lead capture and booking or scheduling tools, and Custom Systems can handle things like online payments and customer portals.

Who updates the site when I finish new jobs?▾

With Website Care ($50/mo), small content changes are included — send over new project photos and we'll add them. It also covers hosting, security, and updates, so you don't have to touch the site yourself.

How long does it take?▾

Most Launch sites go live in about 1–2 weeks once we have your content and photos. Growth sites take longer, and the timeline is set in your quote.

Will a website get me more jobs?▾

A website alone doesn't guarantee calls. What it does is help more of the people who already hear about you — through referrals, yard signs, your truck, search, or social media — trust you enough to reach out instead of calling the next name on the list.
