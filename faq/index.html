What do the care plans include?

▾

Website care is optional and billed monthly, separately from your one-time project price.

Essential Care is $35/month. It includes managed hosting, SSL, deployment management, routine maintenance, uptime checks, a brief monthly service summary, and up to 2 small updates per month. Initial response target: 2 business days.

Professional Care is $50/month. It includes Essential care, plus a monthly Google Search Console review and a monthly performance report, and up to 3 small updates per month. Initial response target: 1 business day.

Growth Care is $99/month. It includes Professional care, plus an SEO and conversion opportunity review, recommendations, and priority support, and up to 5 small updates per month. Priority initial response target: 1 business day.

Plans are month-to-month and can be canceled before renewal. Custom Systems management starts at $50/month. New pages, redesigns, custom features, and ongoing SEO campaigns are quoted separately.
