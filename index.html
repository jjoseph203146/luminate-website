    About

    Founder-led, Augusta-rooted.

    Luminate Web Design & Development was started to give Augusta's small businesses, ministries, and nonprofits the kind of website — and software — usually reserved for big budgets.

    Jacob founded Luminate after seeing how many great local businesses were being held back by outdated, hard-to-use websites — or no website at all. Today, Luminate designs and builds custom sites and business software for businesses, churches, and nonprofits across the CSRA, and stays on as a long-term partner through its management plans.

    Every project is handled personally — one point of contact, real conversations, and a site that's actually built for how your business works.
