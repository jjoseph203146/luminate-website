How is the final price set?▾

Every project starts with a conversation, and your final scope and pricing are quoted upfront. Your exact price depends on scope, and you'll get a written quote before work begins.

Who is the $350 Personal website for?▾

Personal pricing is intended for individual portfolios, résumé sites, and personal brands. Business websites start with Launch.

Is Website Care included in the project price?▾

Website care is optional and billed monthly, separately from your one-time project price. Plans are month-to-month and can be canceled before renewal. Domain costs and third-party subscriptions, licenses, or usage fees are separate.

What do the care plans include?▾

Essential is $35/month and includes managed hosting, SSL, deployment management, routine maintenance, uptime checks, a brief monthly service summary, and up to 2 small updates per month. Professional is $50/month and includes Essential care, plus a monthly Google Search Console review, a monthly performance report, and up to 3 small updates per month. Growth is $99/month and includes Professional care, plus an SEO and conversion opportunity review, recommendations, priority support, and up to 5 small updates per month. Custom Systems management starts at $50/month. New pages, redesigns, custom features, and ongoing SEO campaigns are quoted separately.

What counts as a small update?▾

Small updates are assessed by complexity. Examples include changing business hours, replacing an image, or editing a short section of existing text. Unused updates do not roll over. Response targets apply to the initial acknowledgment or assessment. Resolution timing depends on the request. Search Console and SEO reviews provide findings and recommendations. Implementation is covered only when it fits the included update allowance or is separately approved and quoted.

How long does a website take?▾

Most marketing sites launch in about 1–2 weeks. Most Launch sites go live in about 1–2 weeks once we have your content. Larger Growth sites and custom systems take longer, and the timeline is set in your quote.
