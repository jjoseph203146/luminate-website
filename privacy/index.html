PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy covers the public website at luminatewebdesign.com, run by Luminate Web Design & Development LLC in Augusta, Georgia. Questions about it can go to jacob@luminatewebdesign.com.

What the contact form collects

The “Start your project” form asks for:

  • Your name (required)
  • Email address (required)
  • Business name (optional)
  • Phone number (optional)
  • A short note about what the business does (optional)
  • The service you are interested in (optional)
  • Project details (required)

Please don’t put passwords or payment card numbers in that form. A hidden field and a short-lived token are used to cut down on automated submissions. Those are not stored as part of your inquiry.

How we use it

We use what you send to reply, prepare a quote, and do the work if we agree on a project. Specifically:

  • The inquiry is emailed to jacob@luminatewebdesign.com through Resend. You may also get a confirmation email at the address you entered, with a copy of what you sent.
  • The same details — name, business name, email, phone, service, and message — are sent to Luminate OS, the system we use to keep track of project inquiries.

We do not sell this information.

Analytics

This site loads Google Analytics, measurement ID G-DM2C6EXJZD, through Google’s tag (gtag.js), which is served from googletagmanager.com. Google may set cookies and receive pages you view, a general location, device and browser type, and the site that referred you. We use that to see which pages are useful. Google’s own policy describes what they do with it.

Client billing

“Manage Subscription” opens a billing portal hosted by Stripe. Card numbers are entered on Stripe’s site, not on this website, and we do not store them here. Stripe’s privacy policy covers those payment details. Care plans are billed monthly and can be canceled before renewal, as described on the pricing page.

How long we keep inquiries

We keep contact-form records for as long as we need them to reply, do the work, and keep ordinary business and accounting records. Email jacob@luminatewebdesign.com if you want an inquiry deleted. We will delete it unless we have to keep it for a legal, tax, or accounting reason.

Who else sees it

Form details are shared with the services that deliver them: Resend for email, and Luminate OS for our inquiry list. Analytics data is processed by Google. Client billing is processed by Stripe. We may also share information if the law requires it.

Children

This site is meant for businesses and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page will change with it.
