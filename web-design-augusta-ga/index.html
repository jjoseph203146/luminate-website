FAQ

Questions Augusta business owners ask before hiring a web designer

Don't see yours? Call (762) 227-9287 or email us.

How much does a website cost in Augusta, GA?▾

With Luminate, business websites start at $750 for a Launch site (1–3 custom pages) and $1,500 for a larger Growth site with lead capture and booking tools. Custom systems like portals, e-commerce, and integrations start at $3,500. Your exact price depends on scope, and you'll get a written quote before work begins. Optional Website Care is $50/mo, and System Care for applications starts at $125/mo.

How long does a website usually take?▾

Most Launch sites go live in about 1–2 weeks once we have your content. Larger Growth sites and custom systems take longer, and the timeline is set in your quote so you know what to expect up front.

Do you work only with Augusta businesses?▾

We're based in Augusta and focus on businesses across the CSRA, but we're not limited to it — our work also includes the official site for the Florida Section of the American Tennis Association.

Can you redesign an existing website?▾

Yes. Many projects start with an existing site that's outdated or hard to manage. We look at what's working, keep the content worth keeping, and rebuild the rest around how your business operates today.

Do you handle hosting and maintenance?▾

Yes. Website Care ($50/mo) covers hosting, security, software updates, and small content changes after launch. Applications and systems with databases, logins, or payments use System Care, starting at $125/mo.

Can you build e-commerce or custom business systems?▾

Yes. Our Custom Systems work includes online stores and payments, client portals, dashboards, membership and booking systems, and integrations with the tools you already use. These start at $3,500 and are scoped individually.

Will a new website automatically generate leads?▾

No — a website by itself doesn't guarantee traffic or leads. What it does is help your business present itself professionally and convert more of the people already finding you through referrals, search, advertising, social media, and other channels.
